# Voice profile

Fill only what the author's edits support. Leave a line blank rather than guess. Ask the author to confirm each section.

## Role
Who is writing, and for whom? (e.g. "a practitioner sharing what broke, writing for developers trying this for the first time")

## Tone
How should it sound? Three or four adjectives, plus one sentence of what that means in practice.

## Refused expressions
Words, phrases and moves the author deletes every time. List the actual text, e.g. "总而言之", "值得注意的是", sweeping openers about the era.

## Preferred structure
How the author likes pieces built: how to open, how long sections run, where the judgment goes, how to end.

## What I always add
The kinds of material the author reliably supplies after the AI draft (scenes, numbers, boundaries). Tells the AI which slots to leave.

## Mistakes AI keeps making with this author
Recurring problems seen in the edits, e.g. "turns every opinion into a balanced both-sides paragraph".

## Revision priorities
In what order to revise when feedback is limited. Example: 1) cut empty openers, 2) restore stance, 3) add boundary, 4) only then polish wording.

## Evidence
Two or three real before/after pairs that justify the rules above.
